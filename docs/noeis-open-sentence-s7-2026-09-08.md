## H7 — Carry a small exploration out

Share one question, two passages, and a provisional conclusion rather than an entire Library or dossier.

Carry this out when two inspectable passages sit beside the line. Bound passages stay out until they are included by name. Inclusion freezes title and passage only — not a door, an id, surrounding lines, or a later private edit. The question and the conclusion are typed. Using the unfinished question, or the space between, is an explicit act. Neither is copied in.

The snapshot is live when the question, both included passages, and the conclusion are all present. Empty pending is not a kept closed draft. Clearing the question unmakes it.

The preview is what a recipient would see. No authoring chrome lives inside it. Copy this snapshot is plain text: the question, two titled passages, and the conclusion. No invented public URL, no marketing footer, no library door. Caption: “A snapshot. It is not a publication.”

A source set aside cannot be included. Closing does not publish. Identity `against` is the accepted line; restore drops the snapshot if that line moved on.

Library with only one passage stays silent. Public shares still do not restore a private pocket.
