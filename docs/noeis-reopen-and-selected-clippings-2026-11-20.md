# Bring a task back, take a sentence with you

- In Work, complete a task and wait for its save. Open Done this week, tab to Reopen, and activate it. The original task returns to its open list, receives focus, and leaves the ledger. Check an earlier task and an item inside a folded weekly priority. Reload to confirm the original completion and the later reopening are in the same task history.
- Pending completions and appointments stay readable in the weekly ledger. The reopen action is available for acknowledged to-dos and questions. If a restored task has no row in the current view, focus returns to the Next disclosure.
- In Editions, select part of a finding. Activate Copy selection and inspect the exact selected text, issue credit, and finding link. The full clipping and the lead reading’s Leave a thought action still work. Verify that a selection across two readings offers no clipping.
- Deny clipboard access and use Tab to reach the selectable text. Escape closes the selection actions and returns focus to the finding. Paste the copied link to verify the exact landing spot.
- Check touch and keyboard at 430px with enlarged text, WebKit at 1320px, and Chromium at 1440px.
