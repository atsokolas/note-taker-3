# Noeis — close the loop

## Product promise

Noeis keeps your thinking current.

The smallest complete loop is: **hold a sentence, let your reading talk back, and decide what changes**. Library, Think, Wiki, and Judgment are four rooms in that loop—not four separate products.

Investment dossiers remain the proving ground for consequential research. They are not the product's universal ontology. Ordinary Wiki pages stay article-first and general-purpose; repository wikis and dossiers remain explicit specialized types.

## Operating rules

- Tell the truth before adding intelligence. Unknown, empty, failed, proposed, accepted, and public are distinct states.
- Silence beats filler. If evidence does not bear on the exact sentence, return nothing.
- The agent is one durable collaborator with room-specific posture and explicit context binding.
- Sources stay inspectable in Noeis. Every citation returns to the exact source or passage.
- Human acceptance is the boundary between a proposal and maintained knowledge.
- Local tests, rendered acceptance, merge, deploy, and production are separate proof levels.
- Favor shared primitives and delete superseded paths instead of adding parallel implementations.

## Bet 0 — the product tells the truth

Close trust-breaking seams before expanding the loop.

1. Truthful first paint: never render a zero, empty corpus, or insufficient-history claim before its request resolves.
2. Reliable Library reading: established and fresh accounts load; failure and empty states are visibly different.
3. In-app provenance: Wiki citations and agent evidence open the exact Library source or passage.
4. Exact agent identity: room changes rebind context; no stale answers or raw internal IDs.
5. Clean content and public proof: imported syntax is sanitized, proof grade is consistent, and corrupted claims fail closed.
6. Visual finish: responsive typography, scroll, overflow, and specialized dossier headers hold at 1440, 1320, and 430px.

**Exit:** ten consecutive authenticated production loops on both a fresh and an established account, with instrumentation and no trust-breaking state.

## Bet 1 — the sentence loop

Under a held claim, offer only evidence that answers that exact sentence.

- Why this source, strongest support, strongest counterevidence, and honest silence.
- Name and quote the saved passage; explain why it was selected.
- Accept, preserve, reject, or defer; survive reload with receipt-bound identity.

**Exit:** a user can start from any sentence, inspect both ends, accept one change, reload, and see the same provenance-bound result.

## Bet 2 — reading talks back

Make the loop reversible and findable.

- From a source, show whether it supports, challenges, or says nothing about something the user holds.
- Think is a real place with a durable recent notebook and return path.
- Keep survives reload and is visible where a reader expects it.

**Exit:** source → thought → source works in both directions without losing identity or state.

## Bet 3 — one maintenance loop

Prove that Noeis changes maintained understanding rather than merely generating pages.

- Accept one Wiki review on record.
- Ship one quiet weekly return that either presents a consequential change or explicitly says there is nothing worth changing.
- Send consequential evaluation to Judgment without squeezing ordinary Wiki pages into dossier structure.

**Exit:** one source arrival produces one human-reviewed, receipt-bound knowledge change and one durable lesson or decision.

## Bet 4 — validate the wedge

Use two deliberately different fixtures:

- Costco: a consequential investment case with primary-source evidence and explicit Judgment tracking.
- Parenting: a general-purpose personal-library Wiki that proves the architecture is not investment-shaped.

Run five design-partner sessions. Measure completed loops and consequential actions—not generated words or clicks.

## Detail language

The signature interaction is the **Ariadne thread**: after a person accepts evidence, a fine warm-gold line briefly travels from the quoted source margin to the sentence it changed, settles into a quiet provenance mark, and disappears. It is orientation, not decoration; it must honor reduced motion and never precede a successful write.
