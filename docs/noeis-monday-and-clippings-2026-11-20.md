# Monday on the fold and clippings from the paper

## Work

- Open Close the week using Enter or touch. Plan the first task for Monday and delay the server acknowledgement. Fold the review while the decision is pending and check that the preview stays quiet.
- Release the acknowledgement. The folded review names the first confirmed task from its Monday list. Unfold it to see the whole list and reload to check the saved plan. Verify that an Undo or completion removes the task from the preview and that completed tasks leave the active to-dos.
- A long title should stay on one line in the fold. The full title is in the disclosure and in the open list.

## Editions

- Focus Copy clipping with the keyboard and activate it. The button keeps focus and confirms the copy. Inspect the plain text for the finding, its limit when present, the publication credit, and the link to the exact finding in the issue.
- Paste the link in the browser and check the finding’s scroll and focus. Repeat from a filtered issue and verify that the copied link reaches the finding without the filter or other reading state.
- Block clipboard access and check that the same clipping can be selected manually. Test the existing Wiki copy controls through their shared implementation.
- Run the interactions at 430px with touch and enlarged text, at 1320px in WebKit, and at 1440px in Chromium.

## Small details to try next

- Let a freshly completed Work item be reopened from the weekly ledger. Focus could return to its restored to-do.
- Let Editions copy an explicitly selected sentence with the same issue credit and exact finding link. The whole-finding action can stay where it is.
