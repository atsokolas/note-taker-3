A knowledge base for you and your AI agents

Grow a knowledge base from what you read.

Noeis keeps every article, highlight and note you save, grows them into a wiki that cites its sources, and lets you and your AI agents learn from it. Answers come from your own reading, with the passage quoted.

How it works

Save what you read.

Clip articles with the browser extension, paste text, or bring in your Readwise library and Notion pages. Highlights and notes stay with the passage they came from.

Grow a wiki that cites itself.

Noeis turns your sources into pages on the subjects you follow. Pages cite the passages they are built from, so you can always check the source.

Ask, and get the passage back.

Questions are answered from your library with the exact quote. When your reading has nothing on it, Noeis says so instead of guessing.

Let your agents learn from it.

Connect Claude Code, Codex and other agents to your knowledge base. You approve every connection, and access is read-only unless you grant more.
